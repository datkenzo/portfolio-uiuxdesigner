MYAVIRON COMPANIAN APP

The MyAviron Companion App was created to make it easier for users to connect their Aviron workout data anywhere, anytime. It helps bring the Aviron experience beyond the machine, reaching more people and keeping them engaged with their fitness journey.

MY ROLE

As the first designer on the MyAviron Companion App, I was responsible for shaping the initial user experience and visual direction. I established the core design system, defined interaction patterns, and collaborated closely with developers and product owners to bring the first version of the app to life.

PAINT POINT - DISCOVERY

User Experience

  • Every time users wanted to view their workout data or progress, they had to be at home and use the large machine screen — which was quite inconvenient.
  • Within the Aviron community, when members shared their workout statistics, they often took photos of the screen with their phones, resulting in blurry, tilted, or unprofessional images.

Engagement & Retention

  • After workouts, user engagement dropped sharply — there was no channel to keep users connected daily.
  • Without reminders or mobile access, users lost motivation to maintain streaks or join challenges.
  • There was no platform for notifications or updates about new games, programs, or rewards. Technically, we had email, but it incurred additional costs every time we sent one to users.

Business / Ecosystem Pain Points

  • The company needed a way to expand beyond rowing machines, reaching new users who might join through community and app experience.
  • A mobile app could centralize user accounts, subscriptions, and performance tracking — making it easier for support and CRM teams.
  • It also opened new marketing opportunities, like push notifications and referral programs.

PROBLEM FRAMING

Key Feature

The mobile app addresses these pain points by extending the Aviron experience beyond the machine:

  • Instant Workout Sync – Automatically syncs every session with the mobile app so users can track progress anytime, anywhere.
  • Visual Performance Dashboard – Interactive charts and graphs display rowing metrics in a clean, digestible format. Users can filter by time period, workout type, or personal records.
  • Smart Sharing Tools – Users can create high-quality, customizable images of their workout stats for social media, replacing the blurry machine photos.
  • Notifications & Expiration Alerts – For premium or time-limited video content, the app alerts users before expiration, ensuring they don’t miss Aviron's new updates.

VALIDATION

GOOD FEEDBACKS

After launching the MyAviron Companion App, we received encouraging results both from metrics and user feedback.
At the time I write this case study, the app reached over 6,000 downloads across iOS and Android, with ~60% adoption rate among existing Aviron members. Many users shared positive feedback about the convenience of tracking their progress anytime, anywhere — no longer limited to the rowing machine screen.

“It’s so nice to check my stats and challenges right from my phone!”

“I love being reminded about my streak — it really helps me stay consistent!”

Users appreciated the clear data visualization, quick access to workout summaries, and personalized notifications that helped them stay on track.
The social sharing templates also sparked activity within the Aviron Community, where members began posting their achievements more frequently and with consistent Aviron branding.

FEEDBACKS NEED TO IMPROVE

However, there were also areas to improve. Some users expressed interest in more social interaction directly inside the app, such as liking or commenting on friends’ activities. Others wanted better integration between the app and the rowing machine, especially for syncing data and managing device settings.

These insights highlight opportunities for future growth:

  • Expanding social and community features to increase engagement.
  • Introducing in-app challenges and group competitions.
  • Enhancing real-time connection between the app and Aviron hardware for seamless tracking.

Overall, the launch successfully bridged the gap between hardware and mobile experience — creating a foundation for stronger user retention and community-driven motivation.

DEEP DIVE: DESIGNING THE AVIRON 2025 RECAP

Discovery

Through user observations and community discussions, we identified several unmet needs:

  • Users wanted a meaningful way to look back at their entire year of training
  • At the end of the year, user motivation dipped — there was no special moment or celebration around their achievements.
  • Aviron lacked “shareable moments” similar to Spotify Wrapped or Strava Yearly Recap, which are powerful for both retention and brand visibility.

Problem Framing

Users need a visually inspiring and personalized yearly recap so they can celebrate their achievements, stay motivated, and proudly share their progress with the Aviron community.

Exploration

We brainstormed different directions:

  • Turning a full year of data into a narrative flow with themed sections
  • Highlighting achievements such as total calories, best streaks, or memorable milestones
  • Designing branded, shareable cards optimized for social media
  • Using motion and transitions to make the recap feel premium and celebratory
  • Adding subtle gamification, like badges or “top 10% performance” highlights
  • Ending the recap with a summarize

Design Execution

1Introduction:

To ensure users immediately noticed the new Recap 2025 feature, I designed a push notification to re-engage users who hadn’t opened the app recently, an in-app pop-up that appeared on launch, and a prominent homepage banner.

2Primary Screens:

The core Recap 2025 screens were designed with a clean, minimal structure to keep users focused on their personal achievements. I used bold primary statistics and a bright, youthful color palette that aligns with Aviron’s energetic brand identity. Each section highlights a key moment of the user’s fitness journey — from total workouts and PRs to streak milestones — presented in a visually engaging layout that feels modern, celebratory, and easy to digest.

3Motion & Interaction

To bring the Recap 2025 experience to life, I created simple animations that connect each data point into one continuous story. Since I’m still a beginner with animation, I focused on simple transitions and interactions that can be built directly in Figma and are easy for developers to implement.

These micro-animations help guide the user through their achievements smoothly, making the experience feel more dynamic and engaging without adding unnecessary complexity.
